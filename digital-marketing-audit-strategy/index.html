Case Studies

Businesses Came to Us With Marketing Problems. Here's What We Found.

Silver Jewellery Brand
The Marketing Problem
Content was going viral. Website sales were not growing.
What the Audit Found
Organic popularity was not automatically driving e-commerce conversion.
What the Strategy Changed
  • Separate organic and paid-content roles
  • Build conversion-focused advertising creatives
  • Use retargeting more effectively

Key learning: Viral content and conversion content are not always the same thing.

Ad Account / Creative Problem
The Marketing Problem
The team believed the account had constant creative fatigue.
What the Audit Found
  • Wrong creative direction
  • Insufficient creative testing
  • Inadequate campaign budget
  • Geographies with high return rates
What the Strategy Changed
  • Improve creative selection and testing
  • Allocate enough budget to learn
  • Reduce poor-performing geographies

Key learning: The problem wasn't simply creative fatigue — the diagnosis was incomplete.

Personal Brand Launch
The Marketing Problem
Six months of trying to launch a leadership personal brand — with no clear plan.
What We Found They Needed
  • Personality & tone of voice
  • Content format & scripts
  • Shoot style & production process
  • Platform approach
What the Strategy Changed
A structured Instagram and YouTube personal-brand launch plan was created.

Key learning: Six months of uncertainty became a clear launch plan.
