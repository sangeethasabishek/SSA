Founder & Personal Branding

Become known for the right things.

Customers increasingly want to hear directly from founders and experts. But building a personal brand is not about copying every viral trend or becoming someone you are not.

I help founders, business owners, doctors, professionals and experts decide:

  • What they should talk about
  • What they want to be known for
  • Which platforms matter
  • How they should communicate
  • What content suits their personality
  • What the team should and shouldn't publish
  • How to build authority without damaging credibility

The objective is not simply to become popular. It is to become trusted.

Customer trustSalesIndustry recognition

Secondary outcomes: speaking engagements, collaborations and media visibility. For specialist clients such as doctors, the client approves all subject-specific facts before anything is published.
