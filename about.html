The Journey

From business foundations to marketing strategy

My career has moved across business, banking, entrepreneurship, marketing, consulting and education. Each stage has shaped the way we look at marketing today — not as a collection of campaigns, but as a business function that needs clarity, direction and commercial sense.

2007 — The Beginning

Business & Banking

With a background in Agriculture and Management, Sangeetha began her career in banking and went on to work as a Scale II Bank Manager — an early, hands-on understanding of businesses, customers, numbers and decision-making.

Management & Global Exposure

Learning beyond one industry

Management education, including academic exposure through Cornell University, broadened her understanding of business, strategy and consumer behaviour.

2014 — Building SODME

Entrepreneurship & Digital Marketing

In 2014, Sangeetha co-founded SODME Digital Marketing Pvt. Ltd. Over the years the work expanded across branding, social media, content, digital advertising, e-commerce, websites and marketing strategy.

200+ Brands · 960+ Projects

Learning from real businesses

Years of working across industries meant hands-on experience with different founders, teams, customers and stages of growth. This is where strategy and execution began to come together.

From Execution to Strategy

Marketing Audit & Fractional CMO consulting

One pattern grew clear: businesses often do not lack marketing activity — they lack clarity about what is working, what is not and what should come next. This led naturally to Marketing Audit, Strategy and Fractional CMO consulting.

Educator & Mentor

Making marketing easier to understand

Sangeetha has taught and trained 4,000+ entrepreneurs and students, alongside her work in marketing education and as visiting faculty. Teaching shapes the consulting too: complex problems should still be explained simply.
