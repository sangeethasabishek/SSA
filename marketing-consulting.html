Marketing Audit & Strategy

Before doing more, understand what is happening now.

A marketing problem is rarely just about social media, advertisements or your website. It is usually about how all of them are working together.

We do not begin by recommending another campaign or telling you to create more content. We first look at the business and its marketing as a whole.

02

Our Approach

Business first. Marketing second.

We don't begin with
  • “What should we post?”
  • “Which campaign should we run next?”
  • “Should we spend more on ads?”
We begin with

What is the business trying to achieve — and what is stopping marketing from helping it get there?

Sometimes the answer is advertising. Sometimes the website needs fixing. Sometimes the positioning is weak. Sometimes the wrong customers are being targeted. Sometimes the team needs better direction.

We look at the complete picture before deciding what needs to change.

03

The Outcome

Clarity before more activity.

At the end of a detailed Marketing Audit & Strategy engagement, the business should have clearer answers about what to continue, what to change and what to prioritise.

  • What should we continue?
  • What should we stop?
  • What needs fixing first?
  • What should we prioritise?
  • Who should we focus on?
  • What should our brand stand for?
  • What should our content talk about?
  • How should we use advertising?
  • What should change on the website?
  • What should the team focus on?
  • What should we measure?
  • What should happen next?

The objective is not to create a large strategy document that sits in a folder. The objective is to make the next marketing decisions easier.
